Field Manual

Moving through the forest

Each region is a first-person, roamable forest with hills, dense trees, undergrowth, rocks and fog. Click the view to lock the cursor; the mouse turns where you face, and W A S D walk forward, back and strafe. Esc releases the cursor (and pauses a solo expedition). Animals notice you if you get close: crouch with C (toggle) or hold Shift to sneak — it shrinks your detection radius by more than half. Standing still also helps. Spooked animals bolt, birds take off and flocks follow.

Aim and release

Hold Space (or the left mouse button) to draw the slingshot — the bands stretch and power builds over about a second (the ring around the crosshair fills). Release to fire. Stones fly in a real gravity arc, so hold over distant targets; wind and rain (Weather plugin) bend them. Hold the right mouse button to steady your aim and zoom. Holding a full draw for too long makes your aim waver. The crosshair turns amber when it is over an animal. Head shots count for more.

Ammunition

The sling holds one stone. After every shot it is empty until you press R to reload (about 0.6 s; faster with Outfitter upgrades). Your belt pouch starts with 24 stones per expedition and refills to 20 at the start of every VS round. Stone piles lie around the forest (often near rocks, shown on the minimap) and stones you fire land where they fall — walk over and press E to gather them. The HUD shows whether the sling is loaded and how many stones are left.

Subdue, tag, document

Each animal has a stamina rating. Hits wear it down; at zero the animal is stunned — it drops with stars circling its head, and appears on the minimap. Walk up to it and press E to tag it before it recovers (about 45 s). You then get a field record — sex, individual weight, taxonomy, conservation status and an encyclopedia entry — and the animal is released.

Solo expeditions

Four-minute surveys. Records earn XP and field credits. Levels unlock new regions (Rainforest at 3, Mangrove at 5, Highlands at 8), raise the odds of rare species, and open higher tiers at the Outfitter.

VS Online

One player hosts a room and shares the code or link. Each round the site issues an objective — a species, a sex, a weight class. Both players see identical wildlife. First to complete the objective takes the round (+3). Documenting the wrong animal costs 1 point. Best of 3, 5 or 7.
